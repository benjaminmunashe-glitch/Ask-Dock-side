What you can do

Pick an activity and the assistant will check today's live price, places and wind limit for you.

Kayak tours

Guided tours around the dock, from relaxed beginner trips to sunset paddles.

Stand-up paddleboarding

Find your balance on calm canal water. Great for first-timers and friends.

Paddleboard yoga

A slower session that mixes stretching and balance, out on the water.

Team up and paddle to the drumbeat. Ideal for groups and work outings.

Get pulled across the water on the cable, no boat needed.

Floating sauna

How Ask Dockside works

Nothing is answered from memory. Every question runs through the same four steps.

Fetch the timetable

Today's sessions CSV is downloaded fresh, with prices and places that update every 5 minutes.

Fetch the weather

Live wind at the dock comes from Open-Meteo and is compared with each session's wind limit.

Check the data

Sold-out sessions are labelled, odd prices are flagged and instructions hidden in notes are removed.

Ask the AI

The question, the data and our rules go to the class AI, and the answer comes back with the fetch time.
